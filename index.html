Starkey-Specific Use Cases

How This Works in Your World

Five use cases built specifically for Starkey's hearing industry distribution model and dispensing partner network.

Use Case 01

Audiologist Self-Service Portal

BeforeClinics call or email reps to reorder devices, accessories, and batteries — hundreds of low-value touchpoints every week.
AfterEvery dispensing partner gets a branded portal: browse catalog, reorder in seconds, track delivery, access training materials, and manage warranties independently.
40% reduction in rep order-entry time — 24/7 ordering availability
Use Case 02

Rep-Assisted Ordering at Scale

BeforeReps manually enter orders for small practices, spending 30–60% of their time on admin instead of selling.
AfterReps order on behalf of any account in Salesforce with AI-suggested bundles, upsell prompts, and full order history — in under 2 minutes.
Reps serve 3x more accounts — 4x faster order fulfillment
Use Case 03

Long-Tail Account Activation

BeforeSmall clinics rarely hear from Starkey reps — their spend stays flat and many quietly migrate to competitors.
AfterAI-driven promotions and automated outreach keep every long-tail account engaged without rep intervention. Every account feels like a priority.
Up to 25% increase in account spend — Long-tail retention improved
Use Case 04

New Product Launch Acceleration

BeforeLaunching a new hearing aid model to 3,000+ partners requires weeks of rep comms, manual price sheet updates, and inconsistent messaging.
AfterNew SKUs, updated pricing, and launch promotions publish to the entire network simultaneously — banners, emails, and ordering activated in hours.
Launch time from weeks to days — Consistent experience across all partners
Use Case 05

Account Growth via Personalization

BeforeReps rely on memory to find upsell opportunities — most cross-sell potential goes unnoticed for accounts that don't call in frequently.
AfterEinstein AI surfaces the right product recommendation at the right time — accessories, batteries, service plans — automatically at every touchpoint.
+15–25% avg order value — AI-driven next-best-action for every account
